# Papercraft stop-motion explainer

Use for science, education, or general-knowledge videos built as physical paper puppets, layered cardboard models, pop-up books, or miniature dioramas.

## Translate knowledge into paper mechanics

Lock the topic, audience, duration, ratio, learning outcome, and one concrete visual metaphor. Preserve domain terms. Offer distinct structures only when the user needs ideation: pop-up-book journey, paper laboratory, layered cross-section, miniature nature theater, or mechanism board.

Describe the style as physically constructed paper, not a flat illustration filter:

- visible thickness, fibers, folds, seams, cut/torn edges, tabs, brads, joints, sliders, rotating discs, and pull-tabs;
- four to seven depth planes with real layer shadows and foreground occlusion;
- macro miniature photography, shallow depth of field, and readable 2.5D parallax;
- stepped hand-manipulated motion with small pauses, rebounds, hinged gestures, pieces settling, and background paper mechanisms.

## Storyboard by duration

Start with hook → explanation → example or cultural connection → memory sentence. A 15-second version usually uses about four shots; 30 seconds uses five or six; 60 seconds uses seven to nine. Each shot explains one knowledge beat and records time, visual action, paper mechanism, camera/transition, narration when requested, and sound cue.

## Camera, transition, and sound language

Use slow push-ins, lateral parallax pans, static explanation frames, macro close-ups, slight top-down cross-sections, or layer pass-throughs. Avoid high-speed flights, liquid morphs, smooth CG orbits, plastic surfaces, and motion too large for a miniature set.

Transitions must obey paper physics: page flip, pop-up unfold, pull-tab slide, paper-label wipe, paper-cloud pass, circular paper mask, cut-paper door, cross-section split, sticker/tape reveal, or restrained confetti.

Build sound from the motion: paper flip/rustle, scissors, cardboard slide, wood click, soft pop, tape peel, puppet-joint tap, box opening. If narration is used, keep music light and topic-appropriate, duck it under speech, and check duration and clean endings.

## Prompt construction

For each H3 clip state the paper construction, depth planes, exact mechanism sequence, small puppet actions, physically plausible camera, foreground/background parallax, synchronized tactile SFX, final knowledge state, and continuity into the next clip. Include a compact avoidance statement only where the target interface supports it; the official H3 structured description itself should positively describe the desired result.

## Audit

Check that the concept is understandable, the paper material is visible, depth and shadows are physical, labels do not block the core model, motion is stepped rather than digitally smooth, each shot carries one learning beat, and camera/sound reinforce the mechanism.
