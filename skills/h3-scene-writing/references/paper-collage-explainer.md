# Editorial paper-collage explainer

Use for short visual-metaphor B-roll or explainers made from bold color fields, black-and-white halftone cut-outs, colored cardstock accents, warm keylines, paper shadows, and tactile assembly.

## Convert the idea

For every line or topic, identify the core meaning, emotion, action verb, one concrete visual metaphor, three to six readable object groups, and useful tactile sound events. For a story, divide it into three to six concise beats with distinct metaphors but a shared material and color system.

Default visual grammar:

- clean bold paper color field;
- black-and-white photographic halftone cut-outs;
- selective colored cardstock accents;
- warm cream keylines, subtle fibers and torn edges, layered seams, and soft physical shadows;
- strong foreground/midground/background separation and controlled negative space;
- no accidental readable letters, numbers, UI, subtitles, watermarks, or logos.

## Final-frame-first control

Define or approve the completed collage still before motion. Treat it as the final-frame anchor. Then write the assembly forward:

1. Start from a clean paper field matching the final palette.
2. Base/background groups slide, pop, or press into place.
3. Main metaphor enters.
4. Secondary objects arrive one at a time with small bounce and press-flat pauses.
5. The relationship locks into the approved composition.
6. Hold the completed final frame briefly.

The clip should read as stop-motion assembly, not global fade-in, smooth digital drift, fast spinning, chaotic flight, camera zoom, liquid morphing, or newly invented objects.

## Audio policy

Default to synchronized collage SFX—paper slide, pop, tap, press-flat, rustle, or tiny snap—without automatically adding BGM, voiceover, or subtitles. Add those layers only when the user requests them. When assembling clips, preserve useful tactile SFX.

## H3 packaging and audit

About four seconds often suits one assembly beat, but follow the user's duration. Use the approved still as a final-frame or narrow visual reference when possible; keep the camera stable unless motion is requested. Verify metaphor clarity, object hierarchy, palette continuity, physical paper texture, stepwise assembly, matching start field, final-frame fidelity, and absence of unwanted text/audio.
