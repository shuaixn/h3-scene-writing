# Brand, product, app, or website promo

Use when the film must communicate a real brand promise, product capability, interface, launch, or call to action. Verified facts and identity-bearing assets take priority over visual flourish.

## Build the truth sheet

Collect user-provided originals or official sources for logo, colors, typography, product images, packaging, interface screenshots, features, claims, metrics, CTA, disclaimers, audience, language, duration, and aspect ratio. Record which assets are verified, user-provided, licensed, conceptual, or blocked.

Never redraw or approximate a logo, wordmark, product UI, package, mascot, person, or measurable claim. If an identity-bearing source is missing, omit it, mark a placeholder, or ask for the authorized asset.

## Pick a product-specific story spine

- AI/SaaS: user intent → planning → capabilities → execution → useful output → proof → brand lockup.
- Physical product: hero reveal → interaction → feature macro → use context → result → brand lockup.
- Service/company: context → process → evidence → outcome → promise → CTA.
- Image-led brand: authentic imagery → visual motif → benefit → emotional payoff → brand lockup.

The film must show actual cause and effect. Abstract gradients, particles, and transition geometry may support the story but must not substitute for product evidence.

## Plan exact beats

For a 15-second film, usually plan 5–8 major beats; for 30 seconds, 8–12. Each beat records time, authentic asset owner, primary action, proof/feature shown, exact copy and readable hold, color state, incoming/outgoing transition, synchronized sound, and rhythm intent: setup, anticipation, commitment, impact, brake, or settle.

Use product motion, cursor paths, UI flow, scrolling content, light, object edges, or matched geometry as transition owners. Keep one primary action per beat and two or three meaningful peaks. Preserve logo clear space and readable copy.

## H3 packaging

For a single 4–15 second piece, write one H3 timeline. For longer promos, create a master sequence and separate H3 clip prompts with consistent verified asset IDs, palette, light direction, typography, and motion handoffs. Specify whether audio is native ambience/music or a separate continuous master track; never duplicate both unintentionally.

## Audit

Verify every visible name, feature, metric, slogan, CTA, logo, screenshot, and product appearance against the truth sheet. Check duration, ratio, language, copy holds, final lockup integrity, and whether motion reveals the product rather than obscuring it. If the result is attractive but generic, add a real interaction, verified output, or claim instead of more effects.
