# Minimalist product advertisement

Use for premium short physical-product films with controlled negative space, concise copy, readable product form, and real product-driven transitions.

## Establish product truth

Confirm the usable product images, main variant, other variants, product category, visible mechanisms, verified selling points, duration, aspect ratio, style direction, and in-frame copy mode. Do not infer hidden features or invent logos, measurements, claims, or materials.

Useful style directions include white technology, dark rim light, brand-color field, and restrained lifestyle space. White technology means a clean light field with strong product form; it does not require reflective floors, mirrored stages, glass tables, fake HUDs, or random particles.

## Choose one narrative spine

- Product launch: immediate hero reveal → material/structure detail → natural product action → variants → stable copy closing.
- Feature touch: still product → user/product trigger → mechanism action → detail → result → closing.
- Color family: main variant alone → supporting variants enter → ordered family relation → full-set closing.

Summarize the selected spine in one sentence and keep the main variant visually dominant.

## Motion language

- Drive transitions with product edges, highlight sweeps, opening/folding/snapping/sliding actions, silhouette matches, variant geometry, or matched camera direction.
- Give each beat one primary action; delay text and secondary variants slightly.
- 5 seconds: about one peak and one stable closing. 10 seconds: one or two peaks plus braking holds. 15 seconds: two or three peaks plus quiet detail/copy holds.
- Open with an attractive product action or angle, not empty dead time.
- Preserve product silhouette and copy safe space. Avoid product walls, ecommerce grids, storyboard panels, and collage sheets inside the final video.

## Reference system

When references are useful, prefer three independent anchors rather than one panel sheet:

1. Hero anchor: strongest silhouette and opening direction.
2. Material/function anchor: surface, reflections, tactile detail, and visible mechanism.
3. Final-copy anchor: stable final composition, text position, font feeling, and color relation.

These are identity/layout anchors, not three sequential video scenes. The final H3 output remains full-frame.

## Beat table

Use time range, shot purpose, visual owner, product/camera action, variant state, exact copy, text color/motion, transition/continuity, and rhythm intent. Typical density: 3–4 beats for 5 seconds, 5–7 for 10 seconds, and 6–9 for 15 seconds.

Keep copy short, restrained, and claim-safe. If the user wants Apple-like English copy, propose 2–3 options of roughly 3–5 words and obtain or infer approval only when authorized. At any moment prefer one single-line copy block. Make exact copy and timing literal in the H3 prompt; never replace them with “text appears.”

## Audit

Check product fidelity, main-variant priority, material behavior, readable copy, no fake symbols, no accidental grid/panel layout, exact aspect ratio, meaningful motion peaks, and a stable product-plus-copy closing.
